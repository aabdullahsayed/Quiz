Formula Sheet

QuantityFormula
VoltageV = I × R
CurrentI = V / R
ResistanceR = V / I
Series resistanceR_T = R_1 + R_2 + ...
Parallel resistance (2)R_T = (R_1 × R_2) / (R_1 + R_2)

Section A: Basic Electrical Calculations

1. A resistor has a resistance of 5 Ω and a current of 2 A. What is the voltage?

2. A 12 V battery is connected to a 4 Ω resistor. Find the current.

3. A circuit has a voltage of 24 V and current of 3 A. Find its resistance.

4. A 10 Ω resistor is connected to a 20 V supply. What is the current?

5. A current of 0.5 A flows through a 20 Ω resistor. Find the voltage.

Section B: Series Circuits

6. Two resistors of 3 Ω and 5 Ω are connected in series. What is the total resistance?

7. Two resistors of 4 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in series with a 20 V battery. Find the circuit current.

8. Three resistors of 2 Ω, 3 Ω and 5 Ω are connected in series. Find the total resistance.

9. Two resistors of 2 Ω and 4 Ω are connected in series to a 12 V battery. What is the voltage across the 2 Ω resistor?

10. Three resistors are connected in series. Their resistances are 2 Ω, 4 Ω and 6 Ω. If the circuit current is 2 A, what is the supply voltage?

Section C: Parallel Circuits

11. Two resistors of 6 Ω and 3 Ω are connected in parallel. Find the equivalent resistance.

12. Two resistors of 4 Ω and 4 Ω are connected in parallel. What is their total resistance?

13. Two resistors of 6 Ω and 12 Ω are connected in parallel across a 12 V battery. Find the current through the 6 Ω resistor.

14. Two resistors of 3 Ω and 6 Ω are connected in parallel across a 12 V supply. What is the total current?

15. Three resistors of 2 Ω, 2 Ω and 2 Ω are connected in parallel. Find the equivalent resistance.

Section D: Mixed Circuit Problems

16. A 2 Ω resistor is connected in series with a 4 Ω resistor. The supply voltage is 12 V. Find the current.

17. A 4 Ω resistor is connected in series with a parallel combination of two 4 Ω resistors. What is the total resistance?

18. A 12 V battery is connected to two parallel resistors of 6 Ω and 3 Ω. Find the total current supplied by the battery.

19. A series circuit has a total resistance of 10 Ω and a supply voltage of 50 V. What is the current?

20. A parallel circuit has a total current of 6 A. One branch carries 2 A. How much current flows through the other branch?
